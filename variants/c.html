Questions

What people ask before the first call.

What does it cost?

A website gets one fixed price after the first call. Software is scoped in phases, each with its own fixed price and launch date. You know the number before we start, and that is the number you pay.

How long does it take?

About five weeks for most websites, from the signed scope to launch. Software goes live in phases, so the first useful version is in your hands early and the rest follows on a schedule we agree in writing.

Who owns the code?

You do. The domain, the accounts and the repository are in your name from the start, and you can take them anywhere at any time.

Can you take over software someone else built?

Usually. We read the code before we quote and tell you on the call whether it makes sense to continue it or to rebuild it.

Can our company use Stranko or Belin?

Stranko is for small businesses in Slovenia. Belin is for solar EPCs and the crews who work for them. Write to us and we’ll show you either one on a call.

Which languages do you work in?

Slovenian, German and English: for meetings, for the copy and for the product.
