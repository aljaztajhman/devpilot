A small business describes itself in a few sentences. A minute later it has a website.

Stranko is our website builder for Slovenian trades: salons, dental practices, garages, bakeries. It’s made for owners who will never open an editor, and it never makes up a fact.

Questions

05 / 06

What clients ask before the first call.

What does it cost?

A website gets a written price after the first call. Software is scoped in phases, each with its own price and launch date. You know the number before we start.

How long does it take?

About five weeks for most websites, from signed scope to launch. Software goes live in phases, so the first useful version reaches you early and the rest follows on a schedule agreed in writing.

Who owns the code?

You do. The domain, the accounts and the repository are in your name from the start, and you can take them anywhere at any time.

Can you take over software someone else built?

Usually. We read the code before we quote and tell you whether it makes sense to continue it or rebuild it.

Can our company use Stranko or Belin?

Stranko is for small businesses in Slovenia. Belin is for solar EPC contractors and the crews who work for them. Write to us and we’ll show you either one on a call.

Which languages do you work in?

Slovenian, German and English, for meetings, copy and the product itself.
